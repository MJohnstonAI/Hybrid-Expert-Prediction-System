# E0016 Findings — Physics Shadow Taste Test

## Executive result

The post-June-only taste test supports **three narrow survivals** and rejects broad physics expansion.

- Main: retain `MAIN_NONEQUILIBRIUM_CURRENT` as a cheap candidate-funnel shadow score.
- Main: retain `MAIN_LEVY_TAIL_DIAGNOSTIC` only as a tail-risk diagnostic with zero candidate authority.
- XTRA: retain `XTRA_RICHARDSON_PAIR_DISPERSION` as a prospective candidate-funnel shadow expert.

No pre-June 2026 data were used in the taste test or may be used by these experts.

## 1. Main nonequilibrium current

The exploratory post-June replay improved K13 winner-coordinate capture from 19 to 25 across 17 scored targets and improved mean winner rank from about 27.48 to 26.08. On the two final replay holdouts, K13 capture improved from 1 to 2 winner coordinates.

This is directionally useful but not statistically decisive: the random K13 expectation was about 22.1 coordinates and the approximate upper-tail probability for 25 or more was about 0.26. A separate chronology-irreversibility permutation diagnostic was also not unusual (about p=0.33).

**Interpretation:** the score may contain useful local directional information, but there is no evidence for a broad physical claim that the lottery is a driven nonequilibrium system. Admit the operator as a low-cost shadow score only.

## 2. Main Lévy/anomalous tail model

The heavy-tail model captured 4/5 winning mains inside Top10, Top13 and Top20 on the 2026-08-21 tail draw `2,4,5,24,49`. However, across the 17-target post-June replay it captured 22 K13 winner coordinates, essentially the 22.1 random expectation, and a fixed-K rescue comparison did not improve overall recall.

**Interpretation:** the 2026-08-21 result is useful evidence that ordinary concentrated displacement models can badly underrepresent legal tail events, but it is not evidence that a Lévy ranker predicts future mains. Preserve a heavy-tail diagnostic so HEPS can measure tail pressure; do not let it add candidates, change K, or vote in the normal candidate ensemble.

## 3. XTRA Richardson pair dispersion

This was the strongest physics-derived candidate result in the taste test.

Across 16 post-June XTRA targets:

- K13 winner coordinates improved from 15 to 25;
- K20 improved from 29 to 34;
- mean winner rank improved from about 26.31 to 24.69;
- the two final canonical replay holdouts improved combined K13 capture from 2 to 4 winner coordinates.

The random K13 expectation was about 20.8 and the approximate upper-tail probability for the observed K13 total was about 0.16, so the result is not confirmatory.

**Interpretation:** Richardson-style pair-separation dynamics are worth preserving in XTRA because they attack the actual candidate-acquisition bottleneck and show consistent directional improvement across several metrics. They remain shadow-only until prospective results accumulate.

## 4. Why Richardson is integrated distribution-first

A naive Richardson implementation would forecast gap expansion/contraction and then hard-filter exact numbers. E0016 explicitly rejects that design.

The XTRA implementation instead:

1. models all ten sorted-slot pair separations;
2. shrinks each conditional next-separation distribution toward the exact 5/50 structural pair-separation distribution;
3. converts only the residual information into pair-compatibility messages;
4. propagates those messages into the existing XTRA slot-marginal probability field;
5. preserves exact-slot and anywhere-coordinate probabilities separately;
6. forms only counterfactual shadow K13/K20 baskets at fixed exposure.

This makes Richardson a complement to E0009/E0011-style probability-first routing rather than a competing hard gate.

## 5. Rejected physics additions

The championship did **not** justify routine HEPS integration of:

- a general drift/diffusion tensor;
- optimal-transport main-number flow;
- nucleation as an augmentation of E0013;
- heat-kernel diffusion as an augmentation of E0013;
- Lévy/anomalous diffusion as a normal Main candidate ranker;
- physics-derived XTRA PowerBall models from this family.

These ideas either failed holdout tests, failed to improve the incumbent stage, or added computational complexity without stable lift.

## 6. Computational policy

The surviving experts must remain cheap:

- Main current uses small transition-count matrices and simple vector operations.
- Main Lévy diagnostic uses exact structural displacement masses and a five-value alpha grid.
- XTRA Richardson uses ten pair-separation states and one-pass probability-message updates.

No LLM call is required inside any numerical loop.

## 7. Strongest counterarguments

- The active mechanical-era samples remain very small.
- The taste test is retrospective discovery, even though each replay step is target-blind.
- The physics terminology is an analogy to mathematical operators; sorted lottery coordinates are not literal particle trajectories.
- XTRA Richardson may simply be a small-sample regularizer of slot geometry rather than durable temporal information.
- Main current may proxy recency/frequency unless future residualization shows incremental value.

## Recommendation

Freeze E0016 exactly as specified in `protocol.yaml`. Run the three survivors prospectively in shadow. Do not modify production K, candidate authority, E0013, or PowerBall authority. Require matched-exposure and proper baseline comparisons on every future target.
