# E0006 Findings

## Methodological verdict

Gemini's JOS-HDR exact-line probability formulation is `REJECT`.

Under `NULL_GAP_DM`, every legal six-gap composition is equiprobable and corresponds one-to-one with a legal 5/50 line. Therefore an exact-line highest-density region cannot isolate a high-probability subset under the IID structural null.

A fixed K22 without genuine predictive information captures all five winners with probability only about 1.243%.

The reported Gemini 7/10 K22 5/5 backtest is also rejected as evidence because five listed target vectors conflict with the canonical HEPS ledger.

## Research retained

The underlying concern—independently plausible slots can form poor joint states—is legitimate. E0006 therefore preserves a repaired research direction: exact-null-adjusted joint **feature-class residuals** and temporal state transitions.

## Strongest supporting argument

Joint movement features can be evaluated against exact combinatorial base rates without pretending exact lines are unequally likely under the null.

## Strongest counterargument

With only ~22 active-era draws, a nine-feature joint-state search is highly exposed to multiple testing and sparse-state overfit.

## Likely failure mode

A complex joint residual score may merely rediscover span/order-statistic centrality or morphology commonness.

## Recommended evidence classification

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` for the repaired residual research; `REJECT` for the original JOS-HDR exact-line density claim and supplied backtest evidence.
