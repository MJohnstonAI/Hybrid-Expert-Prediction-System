## Important limitation found

The research document correctly distinguishes cheap exact **evaluation** from difficult global **optimization**, but the current `experiments/E0031/global_optimizer.py` does not implement a non-enumerating globally certified optimizer directly over the structured 5x50 slot-product field.

Its `Distribution` object consumes an explicit table of legal lines and masses; the floating-point MILP/cutting-plane solver iterates over those explicit rows to form cuts. This is valid for the supplied synthetic/scenario distributions and can certify those instances, but it is not yet a proof that a dense full-support 5/50 structured field can be globally optimized over all `C(50,13)` baskets efficiently without materializing line mass.

Therefore the following claims must remain separate:

- **Accepted:** fixed-basket inference/evaluation can avoid full 2.1M enumeration for a factorized slot-product field.
- **Not yet established operationally:** globally certifying the best nonlinear K13 (`4+`, `5/5`, or robust) for a full current HEPS field at acceptable runtime without explicit dense line enumeration.

Any local or multistart nonlinear basket optimizer must report `best_found`, not `global_optimum`, unless a valid bound certificate closes the gap.
