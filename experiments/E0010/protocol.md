# E0010 — XTRA Candidate Preservation, Coordinate Mobility & Triad–Pair Fusion

**Status:** PROTOCOL / ZERO RETROSPECTIVE CREDIT  
**Mode:** `paper_trading_only`  
**Motivation target:** PowerBall XTRA 2026-08-21  
**Do not treat the motivating draw as validation.**

## Objective

Test whether HEPS loses useful XTRA coordinates during slot-specific ranking, K13 compression and final line assembly, and whether a bounded preservation/fusion architecture improves prospective recall and line quality without uncontrolled combinatorial expansion.

## Motivating observations

On 2026-08-21:

- strict FCPC K13 captured 0/5;
- protected specialist K12 captured 4/5: `{17,37,39,48}`;
- S1 exact17 was prospectively predicted through VVD0→10 + HIGH;
- S2 exact23 was retrospectively found to be deterministically generatable by the already-frozen lane algebraic grammar but had not been surfaced;
- coordinate37 was frozen in the S4 secondary field but landed in S3;
- director reports a pre-draw triad `{17,23,37}` on one slate while `{39,48}` occurred together on another.

These observations motivate the experiment but receive no E0010 validation credit.

## Architecture under test

### Gate 0 — Direction freeze

Freeze per-slot H/L/R probabilities first. Human/director HLR observations must be stored as a named expert before the draw.

### Gate 1A — Core 50-coordinate field

Maintain the existing FCPC-style 1..50 marginal evidence field. Score it by composite log loss and Brier versus flat0.1 and structural controls.

### Gate 1B — Lane-specific algebraic rescue

For each slot independently:

1. use only the already-frozen compact grammar;
2. generate every legal next-VVD candidate;
3. map each magnitude through prospectively frozen direction branches;
4. score candidate coordinates by structural destination mass, historical generation/base frequency and dependency penalty;
5. preserve at most **two** algebraic-rescue coordinates per main slot.

All generated values count toward exposure. No formula may be invented after the target draw.

### Gate 1C — Coordinate mobility

Separate coordinate existence from slot provenance.

For coordinate n generated in slot j, define a mobility-adjusted global score:

`G(n) = P_j(n) + lambda * [P_{j-1}(n) + P_{j+1}(n)]`

where lambda is prospectively frozen and must be tuned only by historical walk-forward validation. No credit is awarded for arbitrary post-draw migration.

Track exact-slot and anywhere-in-draw credit separately.

### Gate 2 — Dual candidate surfaces

Do not force all evidence into one K13.

Freeze two independently scored surfaces:

- **Core K13** from the principal probability field;
- **Specialist Reserve K<=12** from orthogonal VVD/algebraic/mobility experts.

The reserve must remain separate and must not be merged into an uncontrolled K25.

Report:

- recall of each surface;
- union recall;
- overlap/dependency;
- random-null recall for each set size;
- proper score of the underlying full fields.

### Gate 3 — Bounded Triad–Pair Fusion

Generate bridge lines only under a frozen rule.

Eligible triad:

- three coordinates appearing together on a frozen slate;
- slate generated by a named expert/human expert;
- triad support above a prospectively frozen threshold.

Eligible pair:

- two coordinates appearing together on a different frozen slate or reserve family;
- source must be independent enough from the triad source under dependency analysis;
- no duplicate coordinate.

Fusion:

`line = sorted(triad ∪ pair)`

Cap bridge production at **5 lines per draw** initially.

The exact `{17,23,37}+{39,48}` fusion from 2026-08-21 is retrospective and earns zero validation credit.

## Conditional order-statistic propagation

Whenever an upstream exact-slot hypothesis changes downstream support materially, recompute downstream distributions conditionally rather than repairing illegal coordinates after independent forecasts.

Example rule form:

`P(S2..S5 | S1=x, frozen evidence)`

not independent `argmax(Sj)` followed by collision repair.

## Scoring

Primary:

1. proper score of full coordinate fields versus flat and exact structural control;
2. Core K13 recall;
3. Specialist Reserve recall;
4. union recall with explicit exposure size;
5. best-line hits among non-fusion lines;
6. best-line hits among fusion lines;
7. incremental gain from fusion over the same underlying slate population.

Secondary:

- exact-slot accuracy;
- anywhere-coordinate accuracy;
- 3+/4+/5-hit counts;
- catastrophe rate 0/1;
- expert dependency and incremental information.

## Promotion criteria

E0010 must not be promoted from experimental status from a single successful draw.

At minimum require:

- multiple prospective targets;
- no deterioration in full-field proper score;
- measurable candidate-recall or line-assembly gain beyond matched random/exposure controls;
- fusion benefit that survives a comparison with randomly paired eligible triads/pairs;
- no material increase in false-positive line count disproportionate to gain.

## Scientific doctrine

The 2026-08-21 near-fusion is evidence that HEPS should investigate information preservation, not evidence that the fusion architecture works.

The question is not: `Could we have formed the winning line after seeing it?`

The question is: `Does a prospectively frozen bounded fusion rule form better lines from the same pre-draw evidence than the current assembler?`
