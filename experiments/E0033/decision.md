# E0033 Decision

Evidence classification: `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

Architecture status: `experimental`.

## Accepted

- Preserve the oracle-replacement backtest protocol as an assembly stage-isolation tool.
- Preserve exhaustive replacement enumeration so no one can cherry-pick which predicted non-winners are removed when forcing all five actual winners into K13.
- Preserve E0022 four-plus-first Johnson geometry as the strongest deterministic high-order coverage component.
- Open a prospective shadow challenger in which Johnson first selects a fixed 20-line 4+/5 coverage set and a predeclared coalition score only reorders those same 20 lines. Reordering may not change membership, K, or line budget.

## Not promoted

- No direct five-line ranker is promoted for exact 5/5.
- No four-node nucleus ranker is promoted. Frequency looked strong on 2026-09-01, PPMI on 2026-09-08, and neither solved 2026-09-04; this target dependence is not evidence of a stable rule.
- The post-hoc PPMI reordering result receives zero prospective credit. It is only a candidate for future freezing and testing.
- Exact 5/5 assembly remains unresolved.

## Research priority

The immediate assembly objective should be split:

1. maximize and preserve 4+/5 winner-state coverage at fixed K13 and fixed line budget;
2. independently test whether a predeclared ordering signal can float the covered 4+/5 line into the Top-10;
3. separately research exact fifth-number completion, because current coalition rankers do not consistently identify the exact 5/5 state even under oracle K13.

A future prospective test should freeze the Johnson-20 membership first, then freeze one ordering rule before the target. One successful target cannot promote the ordering rule.
