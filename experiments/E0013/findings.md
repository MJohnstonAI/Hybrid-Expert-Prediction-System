# E0013 Findings — Discovery Stage

Evidence classification: `PROVISIONAL_SIGNAL`.

The Gemini spectral idea did not improve coordinate acquisition when used as a node selector, but a positive-PMI spectral embedding showed a consistent retrospective tendency to place the future winning five-number coalition above matched random lines.

The effect survived three materially different diagnostics: unrestricted matched random legal lines, morphology-matched lines, and draw-order permutation. An oracle-K13 isolation test also improved future winner rank relative to raw pair count and simple frequency when candidate acquisition was held artificially perfect.

The strongest counterargument is model search: PPMI spectral coherence was identified after several graph formulations were inspected on the same 17 targets. Therefore the observed discovery p-values are not confirmatory and cannot justify production authority.

The correct next action is prospective shadow scoring, beginning 2026-08-28, with the candidate universe held fixed independently of E0013. If the effect persists, the expert may address a real HEPS weakness: coalition assembly after candidate survival. If it disappears, classify the discovery as search noise and reject it.
