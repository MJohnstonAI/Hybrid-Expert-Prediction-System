# CANDIDATE_LATTICE_V01 — Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

A slot-aware candidate lattice that combines deterministic sorted-slot bounds, mandatory HLR direction calls, VVD displacement feasibility and ascending-order constraint propagation can provide a more auditable and potentially more efficient candidate-discovery front end than a flat global Top-K number ranking.

A secondary hypothesis is that ranking all 243 HLR flow vectors by joint coordinate feasibility can provide useful rescue-scenario coverage without replacing the mandatory per-slot HLR forecast.
