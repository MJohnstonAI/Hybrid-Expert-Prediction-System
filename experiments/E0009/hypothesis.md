## Origin

## Problem statement

The 2026-08-21 Main failure was not best explained as an impossible black-swan draw. The realized Main HLR vector was `LLLLH`; the realized VVD vector was `[1,3,15,7,10]`. Several realized displacement magnitudes were structurally plausible, but HEPS had concentrated too aggressively on a small number of HLR and VVD point scenarios. Candidate acquisition failed before coalition assembly.

The central hypothesis is that HEPS will retain future winning coordinates more reliably if it predicts **probability distributions and state transitions first**, then converts them into exact-coordinate support only after HLR, VVD, structural order-statistic mass, exact-state transition evidence, global mobility and rescue evidence are combined.

## Core doctrine under test

`state -> direction distribution -> displacement distribution -> coordinate probability -> convergence -> candidate compression -> assembly`

This replaces the experimental habit of starting with an attractive exact number and constructing supporting motifs afterward.

## Main-field components

### A. S1 Anchor Gate

Slot1 is treated as the first sorted-state anchor for scenario construction, not as a physical cause of later balls.

For each target, HEPS must preserve LOW / REPEAT / HIGH probability mass for S1 rather than collapse to one direction unless a separately promoted veto exists. The resulting S1 coordinate distribution seeds multiple downstream legal slot scenarios.

### B. HLR scenario mixture

Do not use a single HLR vector as a hard gate. Maintain the exact `NULL_HLR_JOINT_243` distribution and any learned residual HLR distribution. Candidate probability is averaged across supported HLR states.

### C. VVD distribution before point prediction

For each slot, maintain a complete legal displacement distribution. Compare learned/shrunk VVD-R against `NULL_VVD_STRUCTURAL`. Exact VVD motifs may adjust probability mass only; they may not replace the distribution.

### D. Tail-rescue reserve

Reserve a preregistered fraction of candidate probability/exposure for legal low-probability but structurally possible displacement branches. The purpose is catastrophic-exclusion control, not retrospective selection of the realized outlier.

### E. Global coordinate mobility

Maintain `P(n appears anywhere)` separately from `P(S_j=n)`. A coordinate with strong global recurrence/mobility evidence must not disappear solely because its previous exact slot changes.

### F. Convergence confidence

An exact coordinate gains additional confidence only when independently defined paths converge, for example structural slot mass + HLR + VVD-R + exact-state successor + mobility. Correlated formula rewrites do not count as independent convergence.

## PowerBall convergence matrix

The XTRA process suggested a useful ordering for PowerBall research:

1. predict HLR state: LOW / REPEAT / HIGH;
2. predict a full VVD displacement distribution;
3. model the historical successor distribution conditional on the exact current PB state;
4. model successor distribution conditional on current PB VVD where sample permits;
5. translate only legal direction-displacement pairs to exact PB values;
6. increase exact-ball confidence only when independent paths converge;
7. diversify when the paths disagree.

Exact-state transition counts must be shrinkage-weighted because the active sample is small.

## Falsifiable claim

At identical candidate exposure, a distribution-first mixture with fixed tail-rescue allocation should improve future winning-coordinate survival and reduce catastrophic exclusions versus point-HLR/point-VVD routing, without worsening proper probability scores versus structural nulls.

No predictive edge is claimed from the 2026-08-21 retrospective diagnosis itself.
