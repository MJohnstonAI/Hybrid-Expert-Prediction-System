# E0009 Findings — Discovery / Pre-Prospective

Evidence classification: `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Finding 1 — 2026-08-21 was a tail-regime draw, not evidence of an impossible state

Every exact 5/50 line remains equiprobable under the null. The unusual feature of `2,4,5,24,49` was its morphology and movement, especially the low `2,4,5` cluster and Slot3 collapse `20 -> 5` (VVD15). The draw does not justify inventing a special black-swan mechanism.

## Finding 2 — Point HLR routing caused information loss

HEPS concentrated on a small number of HLR scenarios and effectively deprived the realized `LLLLH` branch of meaningful candidate exposure. A direction forecast must remain a probability distribution until sufficient evidence exists to grant pruning authority.

Slot1 illustrates the failure cleanly: from prior S1=3, the realized S1=2 was a one-step LOW move. A probability-preserving S1 branch would have kept it alive even if HIGH remained the modal structural direction.

## Finding 3 — VVD point motifs were too brittle

The realized displacement vector was `[1,3,15,7,10]`. The important architecture problem is not that every displacement was unknowable; it is that exact-point VVD stories were allowed to crowd out legal alternative mass. E0005-style full displacement distributions are therefore preferred over E0008-style exact motif calls for primary weighting.

E0008 may remain a residual feature, but its first prospective target was negative and it receives no authority to override calibrated distributions.

## Finding 4 — Tail rescue should protect against one destructive coordinate

The Slot3 VVD15 collapse was the main tail event. The correct response is not to make VVD15 a new favourite after the fact. It is to test a small fixed, target-blind tail-rescue allocation that protects candidate acquisition against rare but legal displacement states.

The rescue allocation must be evaluated at identical total exposure. If it merely enlarges the candidate set without improving matched-exposure recall, it fails.

## Finding 5 — Global-coordinate probability must survive slot migration

A coordinate can remain plausible globally even when its modal sorted slot changes. E0009 therefore reinforces E0007/Q017: HEPS should maintain both `P(number appears anywhere)` and exact-slot probability. Candidate compression should occur after these sources are reconciled, not before.

## Finding 6 — The XTRA lesson is methodological, not transferable constants

The successful XTRA PowerBall process on 2026-08-21 emphasized:

`HLR state -> VVD magnitude -> exact-state transition -> convergence -> exact ball`.

The Main model should import this **decision ordering**, not XTRA numbers, motifs or constants. Main and XTRA remain statistically independent systems unless future matched-exposure tests show incremental cross-game value.

For Main PB13 before 2026-08-21, LOW direction was reasonable, but displacement and exact-state models did not converge on the realized PB4. This is evidence for a confidence rule: when calibrated components disagree, diversify rather than manufacture a high-confidence exact-ball call.

## Finding 7 — Full coordinate probabilities should precede K13

K13 remains the operational compression target, but not the primary information detector. The recommended sequence is:

1. create full 1..50 marginal inclusion probabilities;
2. score them with proper probability rules;
3. combine state/direction/displacement/mobility information with shrinkage;
4. allocate a preregistered tail-rescue component;
5. only then compress to K13 and compare against matched controls;
6. pass the frozen candidate universe to Johnson/coalition assembly.

This is compatible with the FCPC insight from the prior Opus review.

## Strongest counterargument

All conclusions above were motivated by a known losing target. A sufficiently flexible architecture can always be made to appear capable of retaining a past winner. Therefore none of the proposed changes receives predictive credit until frozen prospectively and compared against simpler controls.

## Recommendation

Run E0009 as a shadow architecture starting with the next eligible target. Do not modify `core/heps_architecture.md` or grant hard-pruning authority until prospective evidence and independent review exist.
