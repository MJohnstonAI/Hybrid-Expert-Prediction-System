## 2026-08-25 methodological amendment — E0011

The Ox Alpha contribution was red-teamed and only the valid parts were retained in `experiments/E0011/`.

Binding corrections for future E0009 implementation:

1. The pure structural-null global field is a **control only**. Under IID 5/50, `P0(n appears)=0.1` for every coordinate, so it cannot rank a predictive K13.
2. The Ox proposal to choose tail rescue by `highest null global mobility` is rejected because all 50 coordinates tie under the global structural null.
3. Tail-rescue comparisons should preserve fixed K, initially comparing `Core13`, `Core12+Rescue1`, and `Core11+Rescue2`; rescue seats require a genuinely non-flat frozen residual signal.
4. Convergence confidence must use residual/non-redundant information rather than raw expert vote counts.
5. PowerBall transition research should model shrinkage-weighted conditional successors `P(PB_next | PB_current)` and `P(VVD_next | VVD_current)`; unconditional frequency remains only a baseline.
6. Non-significance means no detected advantage at the current sample/exposure, not proof of a zero effect.

Primary methodology companion: `experiments/E0011/`.
