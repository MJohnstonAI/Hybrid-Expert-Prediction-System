# E0011 Red-Team Requirements

Before any promotion, independently test:

1. The invariant `sum_j P(S_j=n)=0.1` for every main coordinate under the exact IID 5/50 null.
2. Whether residualization materially changes expert rankings or merely relabels correlated scores.
3. Whether residual correlation thresholds are preregistered rather than chosen after outcomes.
4. Whether Core12+Rescue1 or Core11+Rescue2 improves matched-K recall rather than simply changing which failures occur.
5. Whether conditional PB transition shrinkage beats uniform and unconditional-frequency baselines under proper scoring.
6. Whether the chosen shrinkage constants are frozen before target scoring.
7. Whether any convergence bonus double-counts structural legality, HLR and VVD views of the same underlying next-draw geometry.
8. Whether a non-significant result is described as 'no detected advantage' rather than proof of zero effect.

No retrospective generatability result may be used as prospective validation.
