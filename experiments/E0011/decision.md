# E0011 Decision

## Decision

`ACCEPT METHODOLOGY / HOLD PREDICTIVE AUTHORITY`

Evidence classification: `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Accepted immediately as HEPS research discipline

1. Exact structural nulls are mandatory comparators and calibration controls; they are not predictive global-coordinate rankers.
2. Convergence confidence may not count structurally/recency-redundant experts as independent votes.
3. Prospective claims must state a minimum effect of interest and approximate power horizon; fixed review counts are not proof thresholds.
4. PowerBall successor models should be conditional on current state and current VVD, with shrinkage toward simple baselines.
5. Algebraic motif agreement remains diagnostic unless it beats matched controls prospectively.

## Rejected

- Ox Alpha P3 as written: `highest null global mobility` rescue.
- Any pure structural-null K13 retained-mass ranking over global coordinates.
- Unconditional PB frequency posterior as a substitute for a conditional state-transition model.
- Language treating non-significance as proof of no effect.
- Matrix-wide algebraic agreement as an independent vote multiplier.

## Reworked and retained for prospective testing

- Null-first pipeline -> `NULL_CONTROL_PIPELINE`, comparator/fallback calibration only.
- Tail rescue -> fixed-K discrete comparison `Core13`, `Core12+Rescue1`, `Core11+Rescue2`, with rescue chosen only by a non-flat frozen residual signal.
- PB Dirichlet idea -> hierarchical conditional transition distributions for exact PB state and PB VVD state.

## Relationship to core architecture

No predictive expert is promoted by this decision. The accepted methodology may constrain how experiments are scored and how convergence confidence is calculated, but predictive authority still requires prospective evidence and reproduction.
