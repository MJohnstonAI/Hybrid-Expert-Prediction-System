# E0002 — Johnson Covering Assembly and K=13 Candidate Frontier

## Hypothesis

Conditional on an identical frozen candidate universe and identical final-line budget, a Johnson-space covering selector can reduce coalition/portfolio assembly failure relative to the incumbent HEPS selector by arranging candidate coordinates more efficiently for same-line 3+ and 4+ coverage.

This experiment makes **no candidate-discovery claim**. The candidate acquisition problem remains upstream.

## Director decision

On 2026-08-10 the project director approved Johnson/extremal combinatorics as a main-architecture **shadow assembly module only** and selected `K=13` as the primary candidate-acquisition research target.

## Separation of claims

Claim A — geometry:

> Johnson covering can increase exact 3+/4+ coverage of the possible winning five-sets inside a frozen K.

Claim B — predictive assembly:

> On blind targets where the same winning coordinates survive in K, Johnson assembly produces more 3+/4+/5/5 same-line outcomes than the incumbent selector.

Claim C — candidate discovery:

> HEPS can reliably compress to K=13 while retaining future winners above matched controls.

Claim C is **not** supplied by Johnson combinatorics and must be tested separately under Candidate Funnel / Safe Exclusion research.

## Evidence status

`PROVISIONAL_SIGNAL` for assembly; `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` for any end-to-end predictive improvement.
