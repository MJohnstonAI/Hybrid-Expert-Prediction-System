## Component dispositions

### SGCE

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

The revised mathematical specification is sufficiently concrete to preserve as a challenger, but the canonical Main ledger has only 25 active-era draws while the model requires `W=50`. Strict evaluation therefore has zero eligible targets today.

Exploratory partial-window replication shows no detected acquisition lift over matched random, simple frequency, or recency controls. The written k-means/centroid algorithm and supplied row-norm Python implementation are different models and must be reconciled before prospective scoring.

Initial architectural jurisdiction, if implemented: `coalition_assembly` shadow/diagnostic only. No candidate-funnel pruning authority.

### Dynamic Elastic Sum + Polar constraints

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` for prediction.

The formulas can be benchmarked as morphology/compression rules, but current active-era outcomes do not show winner retention disproportionate to legal-space retention. Moving the same filters inside generation may have engineering value but does not itself create a predictive edge.

### `NULL_GAP_DM` filtering rationale

The exact IID gap null is uniform over legal gap compositions. The proposed max-gap constraint may be tested as morphology, but it is not a consequence of `NULL_GAP_DM`.

### Adaptive K13..18

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

K expansion has a large exact-null exposure advantage. Do not interpret higher raw recall at K16/K18 as predictive gain. Continue fixed-K rescue tests in E0007/E0009/E0011 unless a separately preregistered adaptive-K controller beats exposure-matched controls.

### Core10 + Rescue6..8

Do not promote. The rescue objective overlaps existing HEPS work but changes total exposure. Fixed-K Core/Rescue remains the cleaner primary experiment.
