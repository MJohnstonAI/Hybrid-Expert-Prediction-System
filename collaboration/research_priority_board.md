## Gate B — exact structural nulls and probability-first routing

`E0001` remains mandatory for HLR/VVD/gap claims.

## Gate G — PowerBall convergence confidence

Future PB research should independently freeze:

1. full HLR probabilities;
2. full VVD displacement probabilities;
3. shrinkage-weighted `P(PB_next | PB_current)`;
4. shrinkage-weighted `P(VVD_next | VVD_current)`;
5. legal exact-ball translation;
6. residual/dependency-aware convergence status.

Unconditional PB frequency is a baseline, not the transition model. High exact-ball confidence is permitted only when non-redundant components converge. When they disagree, diversify and report the disagreement rather than manufacturing certainty.
