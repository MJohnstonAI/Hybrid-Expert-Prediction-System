# ChatGPT Sol Contribution — Coalition Assembly Benchmark

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** Research challenger; not accepted into `core/`  
**Scope:** Main-number coalition assembly only  
**Operating mode:** Paper trading and reproducible research

## Executive finding

The 31 July 2026 result exposed a genuine HEPS stage separation:

- the human/HEPS candidate hierarchy contained all five winning mains;
- the published ten-line slate never assembled more than two of them;
- a new dual-cluster specialist reconstructed four of the five in one line;
- no tested target-blind assembler selected the exact `10, 11, 37, 45, 46` line inside its final ten.

The best defensible challenger is therefore not a single universal line scorer. It is a **bimodal coalition assembler**:

1. **Eight learned structural lines** generated by a gradient-boosted line ranker.
2. **Two dual-pair motif-coverage lines** reserved for rare topologies such as two separated adjacent pairs plus an anchor.

This improves conditional assembly, but it does not solve end-to-end prediction because complete candidate recall remains uncommon.

## Research question

> Conditional on all five target numbers already being present in a bounded candidate pool, which target-blind assembly method most reliably joins them into a high-overlap or exact five-number line?

This question deliberately isolates the assembly layer from candidate discovery.

## Inputs

Two uploaded historical workbooks were normalized into a reproducible CSV snapshot:

- `Train on Main.xlsx`: 811 draws, 2018-01-09 to 2025-10-17.
- `Train on Plus.xlsx`: 810 draws, 2018-01-12 to 2025-10-17.
- Mechanical-era main sequence: 18 draws, 2026-06-02 to 2026-07-31.

The historical data is used only to train and test reusable assembly logic. It must not be silently promoted as an active mechanical-era prediction dependency.

## Experimental protocol

### Development

- Main and Plus targets through 2023-12-31.
- Each target uses only prior rows.
- One positive line and 60 target-blind negative lines per target.
- Fixed seeds: 123 and 456.

### Historical out-of-time validation

- 25 evenly spaced targets from Main during 2024–2025.
- 25 evenly spaced targets from Plus during 2024–2025.
- Total: 50 targets unseen during model fitting.

### Mechanical-era validation

- 14 walk-forward targets from 2026-06-16 through 2026-07-31.
- Each target uses only earlier mechanical-era draws for HEPS features.
- The learned line rankers were trained only on the older development sample.

### Oracle-conditioned assembly benchmark

For each target, the 18-number pool contains:

- the five target numbers; and
- thirteen distractors selected without target features from the expert-preserving candidate order.

The target is used only to guarantee complete recall. This is an assembly diagnostic, not an end-to-end prediction test.

### Matched random control

Ten combinations selected without replacement from the same 18-number pool have the following conditional probabilities:

| Outcome in ten lines | Random probability |
|---|---:|
| At least one 3+ line | 64.66% |
| At least one 4+ line | 7.45% |
| Exact 5/5 line | 0.1167% |

## Tested methods

1. **Baseline node ranker** — average expert support plus ordinary line structure.
2. **Existing triple-coverage selector** — current greedy novel-triple portfolio logic.
3. **Pair graph** — node support plus shrunken historical pair compatibility.
4. **Scenario likelihood** — empirical sum, register, parity, decade and adjacency distributions.
5. **Role coalition** — preserves hot, stale-hot, void, shadow and bridge roles.
6. **Logistic line ranker** — out-of-time supervised line ranking.
7. **Gradient-boosted line ranker** — nonlinear structural interaction model.
8. **Dual-cluster specialist** — explicit separated adjacent-pair topology.
9. **Pair-of-pairs specialist** — two close complementary pairs plus an anchor.
10. **Bimodal motif mixture** — eight boosted lines plus two pair-motif coverage lines.

## Historical validation results

Conditional on complete 18-number recall:

| Algorithm | Mean best hit | 3+ draws | 4+ draws | Exact 5/5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline node | 1.42 | 5/50 | 0/50 | 0/50 |
| Logistic ranker | **3.68** | **50/50** | **33/50** | 1/50 |
| Gradient-boosted ranker | 3.62 | 49/50 | 29/50 | **3/50** |
| Dual-cluster specialist | 2.04 | 9/50 | 0/50 | 0/50 |
| **Bimodal motif mixture** | **3.58** | **48/50** | **28/50** | **3/50** |

The learned rankers strongly outperform matched random *inside the oracle-conditioned benchmark*. This shows that line-level structure can help distinguish the actual combination from distractors once all winners are already available.

It does **not** show that the full HEPS pipeline can place all five winners into the candidate reservoir prospectively.

## Mechanical-era validation results

| Algorithm | Mean best hit | 3+ draws | 4+ draws | Exact 5/5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline node | 1.64 | 1/14 | 0/14 | 0/14 |
| Logistic ranker | 2.79 | 9/14 | 2/14 | 0/14 |
| Gradient-boosted ranker | **3.29** | 11/14 | **5/14** | **2/14** |
| Dual-cluster specialist | 2.36 | 5/14 | 1/14 | 0/14 |
| **Bimodal motif mixture** | 3.21 | **12/14** | 4/14 | 1/14 |

The boosted ranker is the strongest aggregate conditional assembler in the small mechanical-era sample. The motif mixture sacrifices some aggregate 4+ performance but protects against rare cluster structures.

## 31 July 2026 case study

Actual main vector:

`10 — 11 — 37 — 45 — 46`

Topology:

`(10,11) + 37 + (45,46)`

Roles:

- `10`: stale-hot return;
- `11`: unseen/void;
- `37`: hot anchor;
- `45`: stale-hot return;
- `46`: unseen/void.

Results:

| Method | Best line overlap | Exact line selected? |
|---|---:|---:|
| Baseline node | 2/5 | No |
| Logistic ranker | 2/5 | No |
| Gradient-boosted ranker | 2/5 | No |
| Dual-cluster specialist | **4/5** | No |
| Pair-of-pairs specialist | **4/5** | No |
| Bimodal motif mixture | **4/5** | No |

The dual-cluster true-line rank was 52 among all `C(18,5)=8,568` combinations. Within the pair-of-pairs topology subset it ranked 25. That is a substantial improvement over the generic rankers, but it still falls outside a ten-line slate.

The honest conclusion is that the exact line was not uniquely identifiable from the available target-blind evidence.

## Recommended challenger architecture

### Stage A — Expert-preserving candidate reservoir

Maintain a broader role-balanced reservoir rather than averaging every expert into one score:

- hot and recent;
- stale-hot;
- deep void and unseen;
- pair bridge;
- stiction/shadow;
- structural minority.

Candidate recall and assembly must be scored separately.

### Stage B — Learned structural ranker

Use the gradient-boosted ranker to score all five-number coalitions using:

- expert-support moments;
- role composition and entropy;
- shrunken pair compatibility;
- sum and register likelihood;
- parity and decade geometry;
- gap and adjacency topology.

Allocate eight of ten lines to this champion lane.

### Stage C — Pair-motif coverage lane

Reserve two lines for high-priority close-pair motifs. The second line is chosen to cover strong close pairs not represented by the first, rather than merely taking the second-highest dual-cluster score.

This target-blind coverage rule produced a 4/5 line for 31 July with only two specialist slots.

### Stage D — Portfolio scoring

Report separately:

- candidate recall;
- oracle-conditioned true-line rank;
- best overlap in the generated set;
- best overlap in the selected ten;
- exact and 4+ rates;
- matched-random percentile.

## Promotion gate

Do not edit `core/heps_architecture.md` yet.

Promote only after at least 30 newly frozen mechanical-era targets and only if the challenger:

1. improves 4+ rate over the current champion;
2. does not materially reduce 3+ coverage;
3. beats structure-matched random controls;
4. retains gains under leave-one-feature-family-out ablations;
5. records all candidate pools, scores and selected lines before each draw.

## Decision

**Retain as a challenger.** The experiment demonstrates a real conditional assembly improvement and diagnoses the 31 July failure, but it does not establish a predictive edge for a fair lottery or solve candidate recall.
