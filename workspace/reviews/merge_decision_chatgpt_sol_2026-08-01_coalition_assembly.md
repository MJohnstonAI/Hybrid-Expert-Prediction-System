# Merge Decision — ChatGPT Sol Coalition Assembly Research

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Decision:** **ACCEPT INTO CORE AS AN EXPERIMENTAL ASSEMBLY BREAKTHROUGH**  
**Director decision:** The HEPS director has accepted the Coulomb pair-of-pairs + anchor model as a material improvement over the previous line-assembly architecture.

## Accepted architecture change

Promote the **Coulomb Pair-of-Pairs + Anchor Coalition Assembler** from a research-only challenger into the accepted HEPS synthesis layer.

The module is not a new standalone prediction expert. It sits above the existing experts and attempts to preserve their complementary evidence by assembling:

1. one supported pair;
2. a second disjoint supported pair;
3. one anchor selected from consensus / stale-hot / structural support;
4. a final 10-line portfolio chosen jointly for coalition coverage rather than by independent line ranking alone.

The accepted implementation may use `scripts/coalition_cover_optimizer.py` as research infrastructure and must preserve expert provenance on every generated coalition.

## Breakthrough evidence

The 2026-07-31 diagnostic exposed the previous architecture's central failure: the frozen 17-number hierarchy contained all five winning main numbers `10,11,37,45,46`, but the published portfolio never assembled more than two of them on one line.

The new coalition research materially improved that assembly behavior:

- previous published best main overlap: **2/5**;
- retrospective graph-assembly diagnostic: **4/5** on `02,10,11,37,46`;
- pair-of-pairs + anchor generator included the exact winning line `10,11,37,45,46` and ranked it **19th among 417 challenger coalitions**;
- the exact winning topology can be represented as `(10,11) + 37 + (45,46)`;
- optimized 10-line conditional 3+ coverage from a complete candidate pool materially exceeded matched-random portfolio coverage in the research experiment.

This is accepted as a **breakthrough in coalition assembly capability relative to the previous HEPS synthesizer**.

## Important evidence boundary

The 4/5 and exact-line-rank results above are retrospective diagnostics. They do **not** establish a durable predictive edge or prove that HEPS can identify the exact five-number coalition prospectively.

The mathematical limitation remains: when all five-number subsets of a candidate pool have equal posterior probability, portfolio rearrangement can improve 3+/4+ coverage but cannot improve exact 5/5 probability beyond the number of distinct submitted lines. Exact 5/5 improvement requires validated non-uniform expert interaction evidence.

Accordingly, the breakthrough claim is specifically about **assembly architecture**, not guaranteed lottery prediction.

## Core integration rules

Update `core/heps_architecture.md` and `core/heps_strategy.md` so that:

- pair-of-pairs + anchor becomes an accepted synthesizer-stage module;
- individual expert identities are retained instead of averaged away;
- dual-cluster structures such as one low pair plus one high pair plus an anchor are explicitly legal;
- final portfolio construction uses joint maximum-coverage logic to reduce redundant lines;
- at least three of the ten model-driven lines are sourced from the coalition assembler;
- one chaos/random-control line remains mandatory;
- all coalition lines record contributing experts and pair/anchor rationale.

## Ongoing validation requirement

Acceptance into the architecture does not waive HEPS evaluation doctrine. Prospectively compare:

1. the accepted coalition synthesizer;
2. the prior independent-line selector;
3. a pair/hypergraph variant;
4. matched random controls.

Report candidate recall separately from assembly performance. A selector cannot be credited for an outcome when required winning coordinates were absent from its frozen candidate pool.

## Final status

**ACCEPTED EXPERIMENTAL CORE MODULE — COALITION ASSEMBLY BREAKTHROUGH.**

Use immediately as the preferred HEPS main-number synthesis method, retain the previous selector as a benchmark/control, and continue prospective scoring before making any claim of a durable predictive edge.
