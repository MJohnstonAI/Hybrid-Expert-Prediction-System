## How Richardson complements the current XTRA strategy

### 1. It adds relative geometry, not another point forecast

HLR forecasts direction by slot. VVD models displacement magnitude. Richardson asks a different question:

> Given the current separation between two sorted slots, is the next separation distribution shifted toward expansion, contraction or particular separation scales beyond exact 5/50 geometry?

That pair information can constrain combinations of slot marginals without hard-selecting one exact coordinate.

### 2. It must remain null-residualized

The exact 5/50 pair-separation distribution already creates strong structural preferences. Richardson receives credit only for information beyond that exact geometry.

Never count raw small/large gaps as evidence by themselves.

### 3. It should refine the full field before K13

Do not run Richardson after K13 and then claim candidate lift. The correct order is:

- build the full XTRA slot probability field;
- apply residual Richardson messages;
- derive a Richardson-updated full 1..50 global field;
- compare counterfactual K13/K20 at identical exposure.

### 4. It must not become a hard gap veto

The most dangerous misuse would be:

`predicted expansion -> reject all contraction coordinates`.

That is prohibited. Pair-separation uncertainty remains a distribution.
