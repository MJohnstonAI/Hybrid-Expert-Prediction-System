## Why these lines were chosen

### 1. Distribution-first, not one-pattern-first
The final portfolio does not assume `HHHHH` is certain. It allocates exposure to the leading full-mixture regimes rather than pruning every alternative before candidate selection.

### 2. Slot support dominates arbitrary morphology
Within each allocated HLR regime, numbers were selected from the frozen E0018/Richardson blended slot marginals. No post-draw target information exists, and no terminal-digit sum, board-sum corridor, parity pattern, or decade balance is allowed to override a stronger probability field.

### 3. Coordinate diversification
Repeated structural anchors such as S1=4, middle 22-24, S4 around 33-40, and upper 48-50 are varied across lines to prevent the portfolio becoming ten near-duplicates of one maximum-score combination.

### 4. PB concentration plus hedging
PB12 receives repeated exposure because E0015 currently gives it a material numerical lead. PB5/4/14 cover the next statistical cluster. PB16 is not promoted by E0015 exact-ball rank; it is deliberately reserved for the project director's independently declared `HHLLL | H` reversal hypothesis. PB15 provides one repeat-state hedge.
